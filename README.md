## About This Book

*Yoweri Kaguta Museveni: President of Uganda* is a comprehensive biography that traces the life and career of one of Africa's most consequential leaders. Across fifteen meticulously researched chapters, this biography examines:

- **The revolutionary struggle** — From the hills of Ntungamo to the forests of the Luwero Triangle
- **Economic transformation** — How Museveni rebuilt a collapsed economy into one of Africa's fastest-growing
- **Education and health** — Universal Primary Education, the fight against HIV/AIDS, and investments in human capital
- **Infrastructure development** — Roads, energy, telecommunications, and modernization
- **Foreign policy and Pan-African leadership** — East African integration, the Non-Aligned Movement, and regional diplomacy
- **Leadership and legacy** — The Museveni Doctrine and his lasting impact on Uganda and Africa
